# Pinterest Marketing (Module 05)

## Deep SEO Requirements
You must research keywords online (Pinterest autocomplete, Trends, competitor pin titles and descriptions). Search interest is not the same as purchase intent — cross-check with Etsy sales and reviews.

### Keyword Placement (2026 best practices)
- Pin title: Primary keyword + buyer modifier first (printable, template, editable, planner). Front-load within the first ~40 characters (what shows in the feed). Max 100 characters.
- Pin description: Natural language written for humans. Primary keyword in the first sentence, then 1–2 related phrases and a clear outcome. 200–500 characters is the sweet spot. Max 800.
- On-image text: Primary keyword + short benefit. Must be readable at ~236 px thumbnail width. Pinterest OCR reads overlay text as a ranking signal.
- Alt text: One clear sentence that includes the primary keyword and describes the visual.
- Board: Suggest a keyword-named board (e.g. “Printable Budget Planners”) rather than a brand-only board.

### Pin Formats That Actually Convert (rotate 15–20)
From real selling experience, these formats drive both saves and outbound clicks for digital products:
1. Benefit Headline + Product Mockup (highest CTR)
2. Inside the Product Preview (shows real pages — builds trust)
3. List / Number pin (high saves)
4. Before / After or Problem → Solution
5. Sandwich layout (photo + solid title band)
6. What’s Included (page grid or feature callouts)
7. Freebie vs Premium comparison (when freebie exists)
8. Feature spotlight (targets long-tail)
9. Lifestyle / tablet-on-desk mockup

### Specs & Production Notes
- Standard pins: exactly 1000 × 1500 px (2:3). This is the only ratio that consistently gets full feed real estate.
- High contrast, solid text bands, one clear focal point. Thin fonts and low-contrast overlays kill performance.
- Elevate (do not replace) any existing soft or aspirational aesthetic.
- Generate multiple fresh variations over time rather than 20 near-identical pins. Fresh images outperform recycled ones.

For every pin, output: format used, design direction, on-image text, SEO title, description, alt text, and suggested board.
