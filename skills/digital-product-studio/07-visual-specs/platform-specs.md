# Visual Design & Platform Specifications (Module 07)

Mandatory for every product.

## Gumroad
- Cover: 1280 × 720 px (16:9), under 50 MB, PNG/JPEG/MOV/GIF. Up to 8 images.
- Thumbnail: Minimum 600 × 600 px (1:1). Design separately — do not simply crop the cover.
- Show product mockup, title, key benefits, consistent branding.

## Pinterest
- Standard image Pin: 1000 × 1500 px (2:3)
- Video Pin: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)
- Titles ≤ 100 characters, descriptions ≤ 800 characters
- Text readable at thumbnail size

## Canva Production
Provide exact custom sizes, hex colors, font guidance, margins, export format (PNG/PDF), and file naming conventions.

Always produce design instructions that can be executed in Canva. Prefer realistic digital product mockups.
