# Seller Account Setup (Module 00)

For Indian creators selling digital products to the US market. Practical sequence based on what actually reduces friction.

## Recommended Starting Order
1. Gumroad creator account (simplest for pure digital products and international payouts)
2. Pinterest Business account (required for analytics and promotion)
3. Payment verification and bank details on Gumroad
4. Etsy only after the product is proven (extra compliance and competition)
5. Payoneer if Etsy payouts require it (check current Etsy onboarding for India)
6. PayPal only where it is confirmed eligible and useful

## Accounts

| Platform | Status | Practical Notes |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Gumroad | Mandatory | Start here. Complete identity, tax, and bank verification before launch. |
| Pinterest | Mandatory | Business account for analytics, website claiming, and future ads. |
| Etsy | Conditional | Add only after Gumroad sales prove demand. Higher competition and more compliance. |
| PayPal | Conditional | Verify Indian account eligibility and international payment support before relying on it. |
| Payoneer | Conditional | Frequently required for Etsy payouts from India. Confirm current requirements. |
| GSTIN | Check | Verify current GST registration rules for your situation. Not automatically required for all digital sellers. |

## Account Readiness Gate
Do not mark the project launch-ready until the user confirms:
- Gumroad account created, verified, and payout method active
- Pinterest Business account created and profile completed
- Etsy seller account created (only if using Etsy)
- GSTIN and applicable tax requirements checked
- Payoneer configured if required for the chosen marketplace
- All identity and payment verifications completed

Official resources: Gumroad help, Pinterest Business, Etsy Seller Handbook, Payoneer, PayPal India.
