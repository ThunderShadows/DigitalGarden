# Seller Account Setup (Module 00)

For Indian creators selling digital products to the US market.

## Accounts

| Platform | Required | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Gumroad | Mandatory | Create creator account, complete identity/tax/bank verification |
| Pinterest | Mandatory | Convert to or create Business account for analytics and promotion |
| Etsy | Conditional | Only if selling on Etsy. Check current seller requirements |
| PayPal | Conditional | Verify Indian eligibility and international payment support |
| Payoneer | Conditional | Often required for Etsy payouts from India — check current Etsy onboarding |
| GSTIN | Check | Verify current GST registration requirements for your situation. Not universally mandatory |

## Account Readiness Gate
Do not mark the project launch-ready until the user confirms:
- Gumroad account created and verified
- Payment method and bank details configured
- Pinterest Business account active
- Etsy account created (if using Etsy)
- GSTIN and tax requirements checked
- Payoneer configured (if required)
- Identity and payment verifications completed

Official resources: Gumroad help, PayPal India, Pinterest Business, Etsy Seller Handbook, Payoneer.
